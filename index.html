About me

I am a Computer Science researcher and AI engineer driven by a simple ambition: to build AI that is not only intelligent, but also reliable, efficient, and genuinely useful in the real world. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science (CGPA 3.91/4.00) and a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (CGPA 3.92/4.00) from American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB),a QS 4-Star-rated university and one of Bangladesh’s leading institutions for higher education, where I graduated Magna Cum Laude and received the Dean’s Award in both programs. With 11 publications (published and under review), and multiple ongoing research projects, along with professional experience as an AI Engineer and Software Engineer, I have developed a strong foundation spanning artificial intelligence, machine learning, NLP, LLMs, RAG, and software systems. My academic and professional journey has been shaped by a curiosity to question established approaches, identify overlooked problems, pursue original ideas, and develop AI solutions with meaningful impact beyond the laboratory.
Coming from Bangladesh, I have seen how frontier AI technologies can remain out of reach for many students and everyday users because of their high computational and financial costs, while the growing demand for large-scale AI also raises concerns about energy consumption and environmental impact. These challenges motivate me to explore lightweight, resource-efficient, trustworthy, and accessible AI. My research spans machine learning, NLP, healthcare AI, multimodal learning, LLMs, RAG, and Agentic AI, with each project addressing a different part of this broader challenge. My master’s thesis, LiSTA-NER, focuses on improving AI reliability and effectiveness at the model level by developing a learnable activation function designed for the unique characteristics of NER. My work on adaptive retrieval for multi-hop RAG addresses the efficiency and adaptability of AI systems by exploring how retrieval depth can be dynamically selected based on each query's needs. In parallel, my research on reinforcement learning, Agentic AI, and automatic model optimization explores how AI can learn to make better decisions about its own computation and reduce the cost of resource-intensive models. Together, these research directions form interconnected pieces of a broader vision: to build trustworthy AI that can adapt its behavior, use computational resources intelligently, and maintain strong performance without requiring excessive resources. Ultimately, I aim to contribute toward AI that is powerful, efficient, reliable, sustainable, and accessible to everyone.
My professional experience has complemented my research by giving me practical exposure to LLMs, RAG, Agentic AI, QLoRA/LoRA, vector databases, scalable software systems, APIs, databases, and performance optimization. As an AI Engineer and Software Engineer, I have experienced firsthand the challenges of developing and deploying modern AI systems, which has strengthened my research interest and translated effectively into real-world applications.

Ultimately, I want to build a research career around a simple question: How can we make modern, trustworthy AI available to more people, in more places, without compromising its intelligence, reliability, or usefulness? Through my PhD, I aim to investigate challenging problems across NLP, LLMs, RAG, Agentic AI, efficient deep learning, Trustworthy AI, knowledge-driven systems, and multimodal AI, particularly for real-world applications. My goal is to develop original AI methods that are both technically meaningful and practically useful, contributing toward a future of AI for everybody.
