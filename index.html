About me

I am a Computer Science researcher and AI engineer passionate about building reliable, efficient, and practically useful AI. I hold an M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Computer Science from American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB), a QS 4-Star-rated institution and one of the country's leading universities, with, CGPAs of 3.91 and 3.92 out of 4.00 , and I ,achieved Magna Cum Laude and Dean's Awards in both programs for excellent academic and research performance. With 11 publications , ongoing research, and industry experience in AI, ML, NLP, LLMs, RAG, and software systems, I combine technical depth with a curiosity to question norms, identify overlooked problems, and develop AI solutions with meaningful impact beyond the laboratory.
My work spans NLP, healthcare AI, multimodal learning, LLMs, RAG, and Agentic AI , with a broader focus on building AI systems that are reliable, efficient, and practical. While working with large-scale textual data, I identified an overlooked challenge in Named Entity Recognition: the lack of activation functions designed specifically for the task, where rare entity tokens can be far more informative than the abundant non-entity tokens. This led to my master’s thesis, LiSTA-NER , where I developed a learnable activation function tailored to NER , reflecting my broader approach of identifying overlooked limitations and developing original solutions. My current research extends toward precise, reliable, and resource-efficient LLM systems , particularly through RAG and Agentic AI. While developing multi-hop RAG for gene-function prediction, I observed that different queries can require different retrieval depths, motivating my work on adaptive retrieval and dynamic k-selection using reinforcement learning, Agentic AI, and k-nearest-neighbor methods. I am also exploring automatic model optimization to reduce the computational burden of resource-intensive AI. These research directions address different aspects of the same challenge: making AI more reliable at the model level, more adaptive at the system level, and more efficient in its use of resources. This vision is also shaped by my experience growing up in Bangladesh, where the high computational and financial costs of frontier AI can put advanced technologies out of reach for many, while their growing energy demands raise environmental concerns. Ultimately, I aim to bring these threads together toward a broader vision of trustworthy, resource-efficient AI that is powerful yet efficient, adaptive yet reliable, and accessible to everyone .
My professional experience has complemented my research by giving me practical exposure to LLMs, RAG, Agentic AI, QLoRA/LoRA, vector databases, scalable software systems, APIs, databases, and performance optimization. As an AI Engineer and Software Engineer, I have experienced firsthand the challenges of developing and deploying modern AI systems, which has strengthened my research interest and translated effectively into real-world applications.

Ultimately, I want to build a research career around a simple question: How can we make modern, trustworthy AI available to more people, in more places, without compromising its intelligence, reliability, or usefulness? Through my PhD, I aim to investigate challenging problems across NLP, LLMs, RAG, Agentic AI, efficient deep learning, Trustworthy AI, knowledge-driven systems, and multimodal AI, particularly for real-world applications. My goal is to develop original AI methods that are both technically meaningful and practically useful, contributing toward a future of AI for everybody.
